Agentic AI is a form of artificial intelligence 
that can autonomously plan and execute tasks.

LangChain is a framework for building AI applications.
It provides tools for chains, agents, and memory.

RAG stands for Retrieval Augmented Generation.
It combines document retrieval with AI generation.

CrewAI is a multi-agent framework.
It allows multiple AI agents to work together.

Vector databases store embeddings for similarity search.
FAISS and Chroma are popular vector databases.
